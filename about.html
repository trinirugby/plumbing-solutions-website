Our Story

From One Vehicle and One Plumber to a Regional Plumbing Partner.

Established in 1997, Plumbing Solutions has grown from a small operation into a full-service plumbing company trusted by homeowners, businesses, contractors and developers across Trinidad & Tobago and the Caribbean.

From everyday repairs and maintenance to plumbing design and major commercial installations, our growth has been built on professional service, quality workmanship and a commitment to doing the job properly.

  1. 1992 — Where It Began

    G.C.I. Sprinkler Systems

    G.C.I. Sprinkler Systems was established in Trinidad, specialising in customised automatic irrigation systems.

  2. 1994 — Our First Commercial Project

    Coco Reef Resort & Spa

    Our first major commercial irrigation installation was completed at Coco Reef Resort & Spa in Tobago.

  3. 1996–1997 — Plumbing Solutions Takes Shape

    One vehicle, one plumber

    Recognising the need for a more professional plumbing service, we expanded into plumbing — beginning with one vehicle and one plumber.

  4. 2003 — Expanding Our Offering

    The Bath & Kitchen Design Center

    The Bath & Kitchen Design Center opened in St. James, expanding our offering into sanitary ware, faucets, fittings and quality plumbing products.

  5. 2012 — Raising Industry Standards

    World Plumbing Day in Trinidad & Tobago

    Plumbing Solutions helped launch World Plumbing Day in Trinidad & Tobago, reinforcing our commitment to professional standards, responsible water use and public health.

  6. Today — Built for Every Scale

    A regional plumbing partner

    Today, Plumbing Solutions serves residential and commercial clients across the region — from everyday plumbing needs to complex construction projects.
