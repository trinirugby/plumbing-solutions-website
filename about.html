Our Story

Three decades in the trade

We started as G.C.I. Sprinkler Systems in 1992, installing irrigation across Trinidad & Tobago, and grew into a full-service plumbing contractor serving homes and major developments across the Caribbean — still family-run, with a 24/7 on-call team.

  1. 1992

    G.C.I. Sprinkler Systems founded

    Began installing irrigation systems across Trinidad & Tobago.

  2. 1996

    Diversified into plumbing

    Laying the groundwork for a full-service plumbing division.

  3. 1997

    Plumbing Solutions established

    Grew from one vehicle and one plumber into a full contractor serving residential and commercial clients across the region.
